my approach

My main principle: I work so that your website
makes it easier to tell people about you and your business

  1. (01)

    Design and marketing in one person

    I look at a website through the eyes of both the client and the business. Every decision is based on an analysis of your business and your niche

  2. (02)

    Turnkey websites
    without a dozen contractors

    Structure, copy, design and build — I take care of it all. No need to hire a copywriter, a designer and “someone to put it all together” separately.

  3. (03)

    Transparent process
    and realistic deadlines

    I outline the stages, deadlines and way of working from the start. You always know what's happening with your project and when to expect results

  4. (04)

    Human communication, no pretension or tedium

    You can explain your tasks in plain language, no jargon needed. I ask questions, suggest options and give advice when something can be done simpler and cheaper.

  5. (05)

    Clean, modern design
    without visual noise

    I love minimalism and a focus on meaning and usability. No cluttered blocks unless they're useful to your visitors and to you.
